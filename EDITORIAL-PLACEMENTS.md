# Featured placement

The creation editor and article settings share one grouped control. Single-story slots ask for confirmation before replacing another article; confirmation is checked again on the server. Removing all tags restores automatic selection.

Existing homepage, section/category pins and homepage featured flags are adapted on read. The first placement save persists this migrated map as a private editorial record. Article fields remain intact for revision history. A version check prevents concurrent editors from overwriting each other’s placement map. Drafts and future articles never appear publicly.

Homepage Also Worth Reading prefers its manual pin, then the previous highlight, then the newest eligible story. Changing the highlight records the outgoing story, even while a manual Also Worth Reading choice remains active. Unpinning that manual choice resumes automatic rotation. The lead is always excluded. Scheduled visibility changes use the last recorded lead until the next editorial save.

Only destinations rendered by the site are offered; section-specific destinations require a matching article section. Featured News and Featured Reviews control their existing hero/spotlight; Latest Stories and Latest Reviews order the feeds. Cards, fonts and spacing remain unchanged.

Appearance uses the existing pre-paint next-themes script and storage key. Dark uses #0D0D0D; Midnight uses #000000. Both share off-white text, accessible red, border tokens and dark component variants. Light tokens are unchanged.
